Privacy Policy

Nature Moments Mobile Application

Effective Date: September 19, 2026
🔒 Summary: Nature Moments does NOT require an account, does NOT collect your name, phone number, email, or contacts, and does NOT sell or track your personal information.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Nature Moments was designed with privacy first. You can use all core features anonymously:

  • No registration or login required.
  • We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or social media profiles.
  • We do not request device contact lists, camera, or microphone permissions.
  • We do not track your physical GPS location.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

To provide offline features and save your preferences, the application stores data locally in your device's browser/application sandbox:

  • Liked and Saved Reels: Kept in local storage so you can easily access your favorite videos.
  • Offline Video Media: Downloaded reels are saved in your device's private IndexedDB storage for offline viewing.
  • Language & Sound Preferences: Your preferred language (English, Hindi, Marathi, etc.) and sound settings are kept locally.

This data never leaves your device and can be cleared at any time by clearing the application data or uninstalling the app.

3. Network Communications

The application connects to content servers via the internet exclusively to:

  • Fetch public nature video streams and thumbnails.
  • Stream audio soundscapes requested by the user.

4. Children's Privacy

Nature Moments contains peaceful, family-friendly nature landscapes and does not collect any personal information from children or any other users. It complies with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) and Google Play Families Policy.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions regarding this Privacy Policy, feel free to reach out to the Nature Moments team through our in-app feedback tool.
