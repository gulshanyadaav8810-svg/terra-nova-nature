Welcome to Nature Status.
This Privacy Policy explains how Nature Status ("we", "our", "us", or "the App") handles user information. Nature Status is a simple status video downloading application. We respect your privacy and do not collect, store, use, sell, or share any personal information from our users.

1. About Nature Status

Nature Status is an application that allows users to download and save status videos to their device. The App is designed to provide a simple and easy way for users to download status videos for personal use.

2. Personal Data Collection

We do not collect any personal information from users. Nature Status does not collect or request:

  • Name

  • Phone number
  • Password
  • Home address
  • Date of birth
  • Contacts
  • Device location
  • GPS information
  • Photos
  • Personal files
  • Messages
  • Call information
  • Browsing history
  • IP address for personal identification
  • Any personally identifiable info

No user account or registration is required to use Nature Status.

3. No Data Storage

We do not store any personal user data on our servers. Nature Status does not maintain a database containing personal information about its users. Any video downloaded through the App is saved directly on the user's device. We do not upload or store users' downloaded videos on our servers.

4. No Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share users' personal information with any third party. Since Nature Status does not collect personal information, there is no personal user information maintained by us for sharing with third parties.

5. Device Storage / Media Access

The App may need access to the device's storage or media functionality to save downloaded status videos. This access is used only to provide the App's main functionality. We do not use your personal files, photos, videos, or other media for any purpose other than the functionality requested by you. We do not upload your personal media or files to our servers.

6. Location Information

Nature Status does not collect, track, or store your location information. The App does not require your GPS location or precise location to provide its functionality.

7. Contacts and Personal Information

Nature Status does not access or collect your contacts, phonebook, messages, call history, or other personal information. The App does not require this information to download status videos.

8. Tracking

Nature Status does not personally track users or create personal profiles of users. We do not use personal information to monitor or identify individual users.

9. Third-Party Services

Nature Status does not sell or provide users' personal information to third parties. If any third-party service is required in the future for technical or operational purposes, such services will be used in accordance with their applicable terms and privacy policies.

10. Advertisements

11. Children's Privacy

Nature Status does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users. Because the App does not collect personal information, we do not maintain personal information belonging to children on our servers.
