Welcome to Nature Moments: Peaceful Reels.
This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application Nature Moments: Peaceful Reels (also known as Nature Moments or Nature Status, Package Name: com.gulshan.fullhdstatus), developed, owned, and published by individual developer Gulshan Yadav ("Developer", "we", "our", or "us") on the Google Play Store.

We respect the privacy of our users. This document outlines how we collect, handle, protect, and process information when you use our application.

1. About the Application

Nature Moments: Peaceful Reels (com.gulshan.fullhdstatus) is a peaceful nature reels and video status application that allows users to stream scenic nature videos and save/download peaceful nature statuses directly to their personal devices for offline viewing. The application is free to use and does not require user registration or account creation.

2. Personal Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or sell any personally identifiable information (PII) from users. Nature Moments: Peaceful Reels does not collect or request:

  • Personal Names
  • Email Addresses
  • Phone Numbers
  • Passwords / Credentials
  • Home / Physical Addresses
  • Date of Birth
  • Contacts & Address Book
  • Precise GPS Location
  • Personal Photos & Albums
  • Personal Documents / Files
  • SMS / Chat Messages
  • Call Logs & Phone State
  • Microphone / Audio Recordings
  • Financial / Credit Card Info

No user account or registration is required to enjoy the full functionality of the application.

3. Device Storage and Media Permissions

The application requests standard storage access (such as saving via Android's DownloadManager or public Downloads directory) strictly to perform the user-requested action of saving nature status videos to the device's gallery or Downloads folder. We do not access, scan, read, modify, or upload your private photos, videos, or personal files to any server.

4. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

To support the development and maintenance of this free application, Nature Moments: Peaceful Reels displays third-party advertisements served by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). Google AdMob may collect and process certain non-personal device information, including:

  • Advertising Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (AAID) to serve personalized or non-personalized advertisements, frequency capping, and ad fraud prevention.
  • Device & Network Information: General device model, operating system version, carrier, and general coarse location (IP address at country/city level).
  • Ad Performance Data: Ad viewability, clicks, and diagnostic performance metrics.

All advertising data handled by Google AdMob is processed in strict compliance with Google Play Developer Policies and Google's Privacy Policy. You can learn more about how Google handles advertising data by reviewing Google Advertising & Privacy Policies.

Users can reset or opt out of personalized ads at any time via their Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID or Opt out of Ads Personalization).

5. Google Play Services

The app utilizes standard Google Play Services for reliable app installation, update delivery, and system crash diagnostics. Google Play Services operates under Google's standard privacy guidelines: Google Privacy Policy.

6. No Server-Side User Database

Developer Gulshan Yadav does not operate any private backend database storing user profiles or user identities. All video statuses and nature reels are delivered via secure public media content delivery, and user favorites (likes, bookmarks) are stored strictly client-side on your own device using local storage.

7. Location Data

The application does not request, track, or collect your precise GPS location. Any location-derived information for advertisements is coarse (network-level IP) and processed strictly by third-party ad networks (Google AdMob).

8. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Google Play Families Policy)

Nature Moments: Peaceful Reels is designed for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in applicable jurisdictions). If a parent or guardian believes that personal information has been inadvertently collected, please contact us immediately, and we will take immediate steps to delete any such data.

9. User Data Rights & Data Deletion

Since Developer Gulshan Yadav does not collect or retain personal user data on any central server, there is no personal profile or account to delete. Users can completely remove all locally cached videos and application preferences at any time by clearing app data in Android settings (Settings > Apps > Nature Moments > Storage > Clear Storage) or by uninstalling the application.

10. Security of Your Data

We value your trust and implement industry-standard practices. All video streaming and asset deliveries occur over encrypted HTTPS connections. Downloaded videos remain on the user's local storage under the device operating system's sandbox security.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect operational, legal, or regulatory changes. Any updates will be published at this permanent URL with a revised "Effective Date" at the top of the page. We encourage users to periodically review this page.
